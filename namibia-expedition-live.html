The Core Expedition Laws

Four fixed rules keep the convoy safe, tight, and moving — no fatigue, no night driving, no compromise on the camps.

01

Wheels Turning by 08:00

Maximum daylight utilisation, every single day.

02

Strict 8-Hour Limit

No driver fatigue. No driving in the dark.

03

No Trailing Placeholders

High-standard private ablutions or verified inner-park camps only.

04

Tyre & Fuel Rigor

Deflate for corrugations. Fuel up at every opportunity.

Day 5 · 11:00 AM Departure

The Homeward Decision Point

After the dawn light in the red dunes, the convoy breaks camp and two distinct routes emerge to close the circuit. Both finish on 100% paved highway — the difference is where the weight of the driving lands.

Route 1

Coast-to-Coast

via Swakopmund & Botswana
For the crew that wants a refreshing premium coastal rest after the deep desert heat — and 100% smooth asphalt for the final long haul home.
Day 5
→ Windpomp 14, SwakopmundSolitaire apple-crumble stop + Kuiseb Canyon. Beachside stands, private windbreaks, seafood pub on the sand — no camp cooking.
364 km~4.5 hrs
Day 6
→ Gobabis (Frontier)East across the spine of Namibia on the paved B2 & B6 via Windhoek. Stay at Zelda Game Cabin.
610 km~6.5 hrs
Day 7
→ JohannesburgThe full width of Botswana on the Trans-Kalahari A2, entering SA at Skilpadshek.
780 km~8 hrs
Full circuit ≈ 78% tar · 13% gravel · 9% corrugated
Route 2

South Kalahari

the direct Botswana loop
Bypasses the northern coast entirely to maximise wild bush transit — cutting the final Sunday drive to a short, effortless hop.
Day 5
→ GobabisDiagonally across the central plateau gravel directly to the edge of the Kalahari corridor. Zelda Game Cabin.
560 km~6.5 hrs
Day 6
→ Gaborone (The Big Crush)Sunrise-to-sunset marathon across the heart of Botswana — wheels at 06:30 to clear the border. Phakalane Golf Estate, clubhouse dining.
882 km~10.5 hrs
Day 7
→ JohannesburgA quick N4 dash — back on the highveld by lunch, full afternoon to clean the rigs and wind down.
350 km~4.5 hrs
